Secret Santa Generator

Draw Secret Santa names for free with no emails or sign-up. Add people, keep couples apart, then print folded name slips or pass one phone around.

How it works

Add everyone's name and press Draw names. The generator shuffles everyone fairly so that nobody gets themselves, and pairs you list (like couples or roommates) never draw each other. The results aren't shown on screen, so the organizer can take part too.

Two ways to share the names

  • Print folded slips: each slip shows a person's name on the outside. Fold the blank flap up over the middle section and the gift name is hidden until they open it.
  • Pass the phone: hand one phone around. Each person taps their own name, sees who they're buying for, then hides it before passing the phone on.

Why no emails?

Most Secret Santa sites ask for everyone's email address. This one doesn't need any, and nothing is sent or stored. The draw exists only in your browser, so if you reload the page, the names are drawn again from scratch.

Questions

Is the draw really random?

Yes. It uses your browser's secure random number source. In our tests across thousands of draws, each person was equally likely to get each of the others.

What if the rules make a draw impossible?

The page tells you, for example if there are only three people and two of them can't draw each other. Remove a rule or add someone.

Can I see who got whom?

Not on screen, by design. If you need a record, print the slips and keep them folded until the exchange.
