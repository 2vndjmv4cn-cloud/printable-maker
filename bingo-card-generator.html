Bingo Card Generator

Make printable bingo cards with your own words. Every card is shuffled differently. Great for baby showers, holiday parties, classrooms and road trips.

How to use it

  1. Start from a ready-made list or choose "My own words" and type at least 24 words or phrases, one per line.
  2. Choose how many cards you need: one per player, plus a couple of spares.
  3. Press Make my cards, then Print. Each card prints on its own page, and every card is shuffled differently.

How to call the game

Write each word on a slip of paper and draw them from a bowl, or tick them off the list as they happen (for example, "someone mentions snow" at a holiday party). The first player with five in a row, across, down or diagonally, shouts "Bingo!"

Ideas

  • Baby shower: guests mark gifts as they're opened.
  • Classroom: use vocabulary words, math answers or sight words.
  • Road trip: spot things out the window.
  • Meetings or events: phrases people are likely to say.

Questions

Will two cards ever be the same?

No. The generator checks every card and never prints the same layout twice.

How many words should I use?

24 is the minimum with a free space. Using 30 to 50 makes the cards more varied, so games don't all end at once.

Can I use the cards at a school or event?

Yes. Print as many as you need for home, classroom, office or party use.
