Habit Tracker Maker

Make a printable monthly habit tracker. Pick the month, type your habits and print a grid with the right number of days.

How to use it

  1. Choose the month. The tracker gets the right number of days and the day of the week above each date.
  2. Type up to 12 habits. Small, specific habits ("walk 20 minutes") are easier to tick off than big ones ("get fit").
  3. Print it. It prints sideways (landscape) so every day of the month fits on one page.

Why tracking helps

Seeing a row of filled boxes is motivating, and a gap shows you exactly which days get skipped. Many people find weekends or busy weekdays are where habits slip, and a tracker makes that easy to spot.

Tips

  • Start with two or three habits, then add more once they stick.
  • Keep the tracker where you'll see it every day, like on the fridge or by your bed.
  • Missed a day? Leave it blank and pick it back up tomorrow. A tracker is a record, not a test.

Questions

Can I print a whole year?

Make one month at a time: change the month and print again. Each takes a few seconds.

Why does it print sideways?

Thirty-one days fit much better across a landscape page. If your printer doesn't switch on its own, choose Landscape in the print window.

Is anything saved?

No. Your habits stay in your browser and are gone when you leave.
