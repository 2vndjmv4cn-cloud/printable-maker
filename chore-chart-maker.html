Chore Chart Maker

Make a printable chore chart in seconds. Type your chores and names, then print a weekly chart for each person. No sign-up.

How to use it

  1. Type each chore on its own line. Short, specific chores work best: "Feed the dog" is easier to check off than "Help out".
  2. Add names to get one chart per person, each on its own page. Leave names empty for a single family chart.
  3. Press Make my chart, check the preview, then press Print. To keep a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.

Tips that make chore charts work

  • Keep it short. Four to seven chores for young kids is plenty. A long list gets ignored by Wednesday.
  • Pick a check-off time. Ticking the boxes at the same time every day, such as after dinner, builds the habit.
  • Agree on the reward first. Write it on the chart so everyone knows what a full week earns.
  • Print a fresh one each week. A new chart is a fresh start, and you can change the chores as kids grow.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Make and print as many charts as you like, for your family or a classroom. There's no sign-up.

Can I use it for adults or roommates?

Yes. Change the title to something like "House Jobs" and add each person's name.

Are my names saved anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. When you leave the page, it's gone.
