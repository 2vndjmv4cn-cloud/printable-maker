Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version

Nothing you type into these makers is sent to us or saved. Charts, cards and name draws are made in your own browser and disappear when you close or leave the page.

What we collect

  • What you type: nothing. It never leaves your device.
  • Accounts and emails: there are none. You don't sign up, and the Secret Santa generator doesn't ask for email addresses.
  • Cookies and tracking: this site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to keep the service running. We don't receive or control those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to our shop on Gumroad. If you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Parents and teachers use these makers to create charts for children, but the site is meant to be used by adults and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't collect information from anyone.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising, we'll update this page before doing so, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the contact page.
